# User Research Plan (lightweight, fits a 1-week build)

## Objective
Check that the PRD solves a real problem before and during the build, without delaying the 7-day plan.

## Research questions
1. How do job seekers track applications today, and where does it break?
2. Which of the 7 MVP features would they actually use? Which are missing?
3. Is the board design (5 stages, card contents) clear without explanation?

## Method and timeline
| When | Method | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 5 short interviews (15 min) | Classmates, PG-DMC batchmates, other fresher job seekers | Calls or chats |
| Day 0 to 1 | 1-minute poll under the Day 0 post | Your LinkedIn network | Zero extra work |
| Day 5 to 6 | Usability test on the design or UI (5 people, 10 min) | Same group | Screen share or link |

## Participants
Freshers or early-career developers actively applying to jobs. Aim for 5 interviews and 5 test users.

## Interview guide (15 min)
1. Warm-up (2 min): Tell me what you are applying for right now.
2. Context (4 min): How many places have you applied to? Where do you note them down? Walk me through the last time you applied.
3. Deep dive (6 min): When did you last forget a follow-up or lose a link? What do you wish you could see at a glance? Do you track stages? Which ones?
4. Reaction (2 min): Show the Board and Dashboard screens. What do you think this does? What is missing? What is confusing?
5. Wrap-up (1 min): Anything I missed? Can I show you the finished version?

## Usability test tasks (Day 5 to 6)
1. Sign up and add an application
2. Move it to Interview
3. Find an application by company name
4. Add a note and set a follow-up date
5. Say where you would look to see how many interviews you have

Record: task success (yes/no), time, where they hesitated, quotes.

## Assumptions to validate (from the PRD)
| Assumption | Evidence needed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| People lose track of follow-ups | At least 3 of 5 say they have | |
| Five stages are enough | No one asks for a stage we lack | |
| Drag and drop is expected | Users try to drag cards | |
| Dashboard is useful, not decoration | Users read stats unprompted | |
| Company can be typed as text in the form | No one asks for a company page | |

## Synthesis
Affinity map the notes into themes, then rate each finding on an impact/effort grid:
- High impact, low effort: do inside the week
- High impact, high effort: add to stretch goals
- Low impact: ignore

Scope rule: findings only change the plan if they fit the 7 days. Everything else goes to a v2 list, so the design and PRD do not drift.

## Deliverables
Findings table (themes, evidence, quotes), updated PRD if needed, a v2 backlog.
